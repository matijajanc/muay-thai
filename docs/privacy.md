# Privacy Policy — Glove Mode: Muay Thai

_Effective 14 October 2026_

Glove Mode: Muay Thai ("the app") is a personal training app: combos, a round timer, voice
commands and a training log. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: it stays
on your phone, except anonymous crash reports.

## What the app stores

Your favorites, your current training session, timer settings and saved presets, your training
log (finished timer runs and combo sessions) and your settings. All of it is stored **only on
your device**. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no servers of its own,
and apart from the crash reports below, nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or
anyone else.

## Crash and error reports

When the app crashes, or voice recognition fails with an error, the app sends a report to
[Sentry](https://sentry.io/privacy/), the error-tracking service the developer uses to fix bugs.
A report holds the error and where in the app's code it happened, the app version, technical
details about the phone (model, Android version, language setting, free memory and storage) and a
random ID created for this installation of the app, so reports from the same phone can be grouped.
It contains **no name, account, location, audio, recognized speech or training data**: Sentry is
set to store neither your IP address nor a location derived from it. Reports are kept for up to
90 days.

## Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used **only while you switch voice commands on** (the mic button on the
Training or Timer tab), so you can say commands such as "combo 3" or "timer pause". It stops when
you switch it off or leave the app.

The audio goes to your phone's built-in speech recognition service (for example Google's, or
Samsung's), which turns it into text. When your phone supports it, this happens **on the
device**. Otherwise your phone's speech service may process the audio online, under its
provider's privacy policy. The app itself never records, stores or sends audio, and it doesn't
keep the recognized text: it only checks it for commands.

## Notifications

While a round timer runs, the app shows a notification with the current round and a countdown,
and it keeps ringing the timer's bells when the screen is off. This happens entirely on your
device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
