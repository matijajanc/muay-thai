Combo level markers: wireframes

A combo card shows only its number, name and heart today, so you can’t see its level. Each option below adds a small marker for Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced, and a skull for the 11 Deadliest combos (97–107). All Deadliest combos are Advanced, so the skull takes the place of the Advanced marker. The colours are the filter chip colours: green, amber, red and purple. Nothing in the combo data changes, because diff and type are already on every combo.
