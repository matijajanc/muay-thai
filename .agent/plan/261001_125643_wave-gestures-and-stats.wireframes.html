Wave gestures + Stats: wireframes

Two additions to the Muay Thai app. Wave gestures let you control the timer and combos by waving a glove over the phone. The new Stats tab shows a weekly streak, this week vs last, a 12-week chart, technique mix and history. It uses the app’s dark theme tokens. Plan: 261001_125643_wave-gestures-and-stats.md.

1 · How the wave works No camera. The proximity sensor only knows “something is close” or “nothing is close”.

The sensor

The sensor is a tiny infrared emitter and receiver beside the front camera. It’s the part that blanks the screen when you hold the phone to your ear on a call. It reports near when something is within about 5 cm, and far otherwise.

The sensor can’t see the direction of your hand or its shape. The app tells the gestures apart by timing alone: how long the sensor stays covered, and how many times in a row.

It needs no permission and uses almost no battery. It only works with the phone lying face up, and on Android only for now.

Three gestures, read from the sensor signal

The line is up while the sensor is covered. Long covers are things like a pocket, a phone lying face down, or a hand resting on it. Nothing fires until the sensor reads clear again.

  • Active minutes leave out the lead-in and pauses. For combos, a gap of more than 5 min between combos is left out too.
